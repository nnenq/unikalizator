DESCRIPTION
Costa Rica has no army. For decades it was called the Switzerland of Central America. Now it has built a mega prison based on the blueprints of El Salvador's CECOT.

This is the story of CACCO, the High Containment Center Against Organized Crime: why Costa Rica built it, how it compares with CECOT, who the first prisoners are, and why critics are worried. Up to 60 men per cell, about 1.5 square meters each, biometrics, phone jamming, shaved heads and orange uniforms.
