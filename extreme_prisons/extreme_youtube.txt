DESCRIPTION
A prison where inmates buy their cells like apartments. A prison that chains every new arrival at the ankles. A prison where men walk bent in half with their eyes covered. America's most secure building. A prison with a nightclub and a zoo. A mega prison for 40,000. And a place Syrians called "the human slaughterhouse."

We count down seven of the most extreme prisons on Earth — San Pedro (Bolivia), Bang Kwang (Thailand), Black Dolphin (Russia), ADX Florence (USA), Tocorón (Venezuela), CECOT (El Salvador) and Saydnaya (Syria) — and tell the real stories behind their walls.
