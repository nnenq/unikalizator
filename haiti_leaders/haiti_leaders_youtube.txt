DESCRIPTION
Twelve men accused by the United States and the United Nations of massacres, mass kidnappings and the collapse of Haiti's capital. In October 2026, El Salvador's soldiers arrived to hunt them. One month later, one is serving life in a U.S. prison, one was reported killed by a drone (disputed), two had their houses destroyed from the air, and the rest are still at large or their fate is unknown.

Haiti Files, Part 4. One by one: Jimmy "Barbecue" Chérizier, Wilson Joseph (Lanmò Sanjou) and Germine Joly (Yonyon) of 400 Mawozo, Ti Gabriel of G-Pèp, Vitel'Homme Innocent, Izo of 5 Segonn, Luckson Élan and Prophane Victor of Gran Grif, Mathias Saintil, Jeff Larose, Kempes Sanon and Krisla. Plus the police offensive in Martissant, the first Salvadoran deaths, and the three new prisons.

Accusations are presented as accusations; most of these men have never been tried. Statuses are as of November 25, 2026 and are based on public reports.
