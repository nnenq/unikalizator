DESCRIPTION
December 7, 1989. Poland had just broken free from communism, and its prisoners believed freedom was meant for them too. That evening, the TV news announced a new amnesty law. Repeat offenders were excluded. Within hours, inmates took over the prison in Czarne, and riots followed in Goleniów and Nowogard.

Over three days, guards opened fire, prisoners attacked other prisoners, and militia units stormed the prisons. At least six men died at Czarne, and the exact number and causes of death are still disputed. Forty-four prison officers were later indicted. None was convicted.

This video covers the year of freedom that raised prisoners' hopes, the amnesty and its exclusions, hour by hour events at Czarne, Goleniów and Nowogard, the assault, the disputed death toll, the beatings confirmed by Poland's ombudsman, the trial that ran out of time, and the events behind Netflix's drama series "Riot" (premiering December 9, 2026). Disputed, unproven and single-source claims are marked as such. The Netflix series is a drama, and this video is not affiliated with it.
