DESCRIPTION
On the night of March 2, 2024, armed gangs attacked Haiti's National Penitentiary in Port-au-Prince. The next day, according to a Haitian lawyers' group, only 99 of about 3,700 prisoners answered the roll call. A second prison, in Croix-des-Bouquets, fell the same weekend. According to the Associated Press, more than 4,700 prisoners escaped in two days.

Within weeks, the prime minister could not fly home and resigned. Two and a half years later, after four Salvadoran technicians were ambushed in Croix-des-Bouquets, El Salvador, the country of CECOT, sent its own soldiers into Haiti.

This video covers the assassination of President Jovenel Moïse, the rise of Jimmy "Barbecue" Chérizier and the Viv Ansanm alliance, the night of the jailbreak, the prisoners who stayed, the collapse of the government, Haiti's earlier prison escapes, the Kenya-led mission, the UN Gang Suppression Force, and El Salvador's 2026 deployment. Numbers that differ between sources are marked, and claims are attributed.
