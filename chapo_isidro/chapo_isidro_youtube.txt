DESCRIPTION
Four Mexican soldiers died to capture him. On November 20, 2026, in Guasave, Sinaloa, Mexican forces backed by Black Hawk helicopters and a U.S. drone arrested Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, "El Chapo Isidro", one of the FBI's Ten Most Wanted fugitives, with a reward of up to $5 million.

This video covers who he is (and why he is not the famous El Chapo), his rise from the Beltrán Leyva organization to his own cartel in northern Sinaloa, the U.S. charges and sanctions, the unexplained 2025 San Diego court filing, Operation Centella, the night of burning roadblocks in Guasave, why Mexico remembered the "Culiacanazo", and the fight over whether he will be tried in Mexico or extradited to the United States.

Death tolls and the number of people arrested differ between sources and are presented as reported. Charges are accusations; he has not been convicted. Information is current as of November 24, 2026.
