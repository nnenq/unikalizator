DESCRIPTION
On November 10, 2025, Ecuador moved 300 of its most dangerous prisoners into a brand-new maximum-security prison, El Encuentro. Two days later, President Daniel Noboa admitted the building was only 35 to 40 percent complete.

This is the story of Ecuador's answer to CECOT: the prison massacres that made it, the "300 days" promise, the "four to five weeks" promise, the secret transfer the day after a prison massacre and a week before a national referendum, life inside under the new regime, the deaths and legal complaints that followed, and how it compares with El Salvador's CECOT.
