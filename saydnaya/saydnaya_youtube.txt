DESCRIPTION
On December 8, 2024, as the Assad regime collapsed, rebels and families reached Saydnaya military prison north of Damascus — a place human rights groups called "the human slaughterhouse." Thousands walked out. Some did not know their own names. Some believed Hafez al-Assad, dead for 24 years, was still president.

This documentary tells the story of Saydnaya: how it was built, who was held there, the 2008 riot, Amnesty International's 2017 report estimating 5,000–13,000 executions between 2011 and 2015, the Caesar photographs, the day the doors opened, the desperate search for hidden underground cells, and the families still looking for the missing. Disputed and unverified claims are clearly marked as such.
