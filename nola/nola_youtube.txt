DESCRIPTION
Just after midnight on May 16, 2025, ten men walked out of the Orleans Parish jail in New Orleans. They pulled a cell door off its track, ripped a toilet out of the wall, crawled through the hole behind it, and left a message above it: "To Easy LoL." Nobody noticed for more than seven hours.

This is the full story: the jail with faulty locks, the night of the escape on real surveillance footage, the headcount, the manhunt from the French Quarter to Texas, the friends and relatives who helped, the maintenance worker who turned off the water, the five-month hunt for the last fugitive that ended in an Atlanta crawl space, and the fallout — including the October 2026 sentencing and the indictment of the former sheriff.
