DESCRIPTION
In March 2024, Jimmy "Barbecue" Chérizier warned that if Haiti's prime minister did not resign, it would lead to "civil war" and "genocide." Weeks later, the prime minister was gone. A former police officer, accused of massacres he denies, Chérizier built an alliance of gangs that blockaded the capital's fuel, helped empty its prisons, and now faces U.S. terrorism designations, a $5 million reward and El Salvador's soldiers on the streets of Port-au-Prince.

This video covers his childhood in Delmas, his time in the Haitian National Police, the La Saline massacre and the U.S. Treasury's findings, the G9 alliance, the 2022 fuel blockade, Viv Ansanm and the fall of Ariel Henry, U.S. sanctions and charges, the drone strikes on his homes, and El Salvador's 2026 deployment. Accusations against him are presented as accusations; he denies them, and he has never been tried.
