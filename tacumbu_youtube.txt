DESCRIPTION
At five in the morning on December 18, 2023, more than 2,000 police officers and soldiers surrounded Tacumbú, Paraguay's biggest prison, in the middle of the capital, Asunción. They were there to take it back from the Rotela Clan, the gang that, by many accounts, had been running it.

This video covers how Paraguay became a target for organized crime, the rise of the Rotela Clan and its war with Brazil's PCC, the October 2023 hostage siege, the fighting rooster found in a search, Operation Veneratio, what happened to Armando Javier Rotela, the 2026 riot, the renaming of Tacumbú, and whether a country can take back a prison without fixing the system around it. Figures that differ between sources are marked, and official claims are attributed.
