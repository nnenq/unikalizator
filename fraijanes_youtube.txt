DESCRIPTION
On October 8, 2025, more than 500 police officers searched the Fraijanes II prison outside Guatemala City and counted 256 prisoners. The total matched. Three days later, 20 members of Barrio 18, a gang the United States had just designated a terrorist organization, were missing. There was no tunnel and no broken wall.

One year later, we follow every one of the 20: the ten who were caught (two of them in Cancún, Mexico), the one who was found shot dead, and the nine who, as of our latest check, have not been reported caught. We also cover how prosecutors believe they got out, the guards and officials who were charged, the January 2026 prison riots with 46 hostages, and what Guatemala plans next.

Disputed, unproven and unverified claims are marked as such. Allegations against prison staff and officials have not been proven in court. Status of each fugitive is based on official announcements and Guatemalan media reports up to October 2026.
