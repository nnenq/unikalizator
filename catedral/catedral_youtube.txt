DESCRIPTION
In 1991, Pablo Escobar agreed to go to prison. But first, he got to build it.

La Catedral stood on a mountain above Medellín, with a football pitch, a bar, a jacuzzi, a waterfall and guards Escobar chose himself. Thirteen months later, after murders inside its walls and a botched government transfer, he walked out of it. This is the story of the extradition war, the deal that created La Catedral, life inside "Hotel Escobar", the escape, the manhunt, and what remains of the prison today. Disputed and unverified claims are marked as such.
