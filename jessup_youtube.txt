DESCRIPTION
In its final week, Maryland moved 655 prisoners out of one prison. Some buses left at night. Most of the officers who worked there were told only at the very end. Then, on March 19, 2007, the governor announced that one of the oldest maximum-security prisons in America was already closed.

This is the story of the Maryland House of Correction in Jessup — "The Cut": a 19th-century building with blind spots, radio dead zones and locks inmates could jimmy; the killing of Officer David McGuinn in 2006; the new corrections secretary who toured it and said he had never seen anything that bad; and the need-to-know operation that emptied it before anyone could react.
