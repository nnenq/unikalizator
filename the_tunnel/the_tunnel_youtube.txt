DESCRIPTION
At 8:52 p.m. on July 11, 2015, a camera inside Altiplano, Mexico's most secure prison, watched Joaquín "El Chapo" Guzmán walk behind a low wall in the shower area of his cell. He never came back into view. Under the shower was a hole, a 10-meter shaft, and a lit, ventilated tunnel about 1.5 kilometers long — with a motorcycle inside.

This video reconstructs the escape step by step: Altiplano and its reputation, his first escape from Puente Grande in 2001, his capture in Mazatlán in 2014, the shower blind spot, the tunnel, the political fallout, Operation Black Swan in Los Mochis, his extradition and trial in Brooklyn, ADX Florence — and why Altiplano is back in the news in 2026 with "El Chapo Isidro".

All 3D scenes are schematic reconstructions based on published reports, not exact plans. Disputed details are presented as reported.
