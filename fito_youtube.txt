DESCRIPTION
On January 7, 2024, Ecuadorian soldiers arrived to move the country's most powerful gang leader to a maximum-security prison. His cell was empty. Within 48 hours, a TV studio was stormed live on air and Ecuador declared an "internal armed conflict" against the gangs.

José Adolfo Macías Villamar, known as "Fito," leader of Los Choneros, stayed on the run for 535 days. In June 2025 he was found hiding underground in his hometown of Manta. Weeks later he became the first Ecuadorian extradited by his own country to the United States, where he has pleaded not guilty in federal court in Brooklyn.

This video covers the rise of Los Choneros, the 2013 La Roca escape, the life Fito reportedly lived inside Guayaquil's Regional prison, the killing of presidential candidate Fernando Villavicencio, the unexplained escape, the January 2024 crisis, the manhunt, the bunker in Manta and the case in New York. Disputed, unproven and unverified claims are marked as such. Fito has never been convicted of any role in Villavicencio's killing, and the U.S. charges against him are allegations.
