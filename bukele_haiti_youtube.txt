DESCRIPTION
On October 18, 2026, four Salvadoran Air Force technicians were ambushed and captured near Port-au-Prince. Three days later, President Nayib Bukele promised to bring them back alive, or "finish" Haiti's gangs. Within a week, El Salvador said 2,000 soldiers were in Haiti, most of them outside the UN-backed force.

Haiti Files, Part 3. This video covers the first month: the ambush in Croix-des-Bouquets, the deployment, the first operations in Tabarre and the Plaine du Cul-de-Sac, the friction with Washington, the drone and helicopter strikes on gang leaders' houses, the base in the national stadium, the dead and the disputed numbers, the plan to build three new prisons after Haiti's justice minister toured CECOT, and the four men who have still not come home.

Figures that differ between sources are shown as reported. Claims by governments and gangs are attributed. Information is current as of November 22, 2026.
