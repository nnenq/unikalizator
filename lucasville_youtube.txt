DESCRIPTION
The prisoners had the keys. The guards were hostages. For eleven days, the state of Ohio negotiated to get its prison back.

Easter Sunday, 1993. At the Southern Ohio Correctional Facility in Lucasville, hundreds of inmates take over L block. Three rival gangs form an alliance, twelve officers are held hostage over the course of the siege, and by the end nine prisoners and one corrections officer are dead. This is the full story: the tuberculosis test that started it, the first night, why the state didn't storm the block, the threat on a bedsheet, the live broadcasts, the 21-point surrender, the Lucasville Five, and what changed afterwards. Disputed and unverified claims are marked as such.
