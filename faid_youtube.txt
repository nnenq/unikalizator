DESCRIPTION
July 1, 2018. A small helicopter drops into the courtyard of Réau prison, south east of Paris. Armed men, smoke grenades, an angle grinder. Seven minutes and thirty three seconds later, Rédoine Faïd is gone.

It was his second escape in five years. This is the story of the robber from Creil who said he learned his craft from the film Heat, the 2010 shootout that killed police officer Aurélie Fouquet, the explosive escape from Sequedin prison, the one courtyard without anti-helicopter nets, the 94-day manhunt, the burqa, and the verdict.
