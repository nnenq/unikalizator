DESCRIPTION
A life sentence is one thing. Serving it above the Arctic Circle is another.

In Kharp, a village in Russia's Yamal-Nenets region whose name means "northern lights", two prison colonies stand just down the road from each other. In one, Polar Wolf, Alexei Navalny died in February 2024. The other, Polar Owl (IK-18), holds men sentenced to life, among them, according to Russian media, some of the country's most notorious killers.

This video covers the Arctic location and climate, the Gulag railway that built the village, how life sentences work in Russia (cells, walks, visits and the 10-year steps set out in law), the reported inmates, the false-confession scandal, Navalny's death next door, and the 2026 allegations of torture and missing camera footage, reported by The Insider from a prosecutor's filing.

Allegations are presented as allegations. The prosecutor's filing does not conclude who caused the prisoners' injuries, and the torture allegations have not been proven in court. Reported inmates have not been confirmed by Russia's prison service.
