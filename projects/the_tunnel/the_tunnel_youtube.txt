DESCRIPTION
At 8:52 p.m. on July 11, 2015, a camera inside Altiplano, Mexico's most secure prison, watched Joaquín "El Chapo" Guzmán walk behind a low wall in the shower area of his cell. He never came back into view. Under the shower was a hole, a 10-meter shaft, and a lit, ventilated tunnel about 1.5 kilometers long — with a motorcycle inside.

From a poor village in the mountains of Sinaloa to the first drug tunnels under the U.S. border, two escapes from maximum-security prisons, Operation Black Swan, a trial in Brooklyn and a cell in ADX Florence — El Chapo and his tunnels, step by step. And why Altiplano is back in the news in 2026 with "El Chapo Isidro".

3D scenes are schematic reconstructions based on published reports, not exact plans. Disputed details are presented as reported.
